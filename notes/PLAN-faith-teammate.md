Status: planning only. Nothing built. Name not chosen (options offered: Asa, Lev, Ira, Noa, Ari, Sol).

## What the teammate owns

1. **The devotional year.** Reworking the full year Bart has written, one month at a time, with him reviewing every day before it is scheduled.
2. **The devotional Facebook Page.** Scheduling each day to post automatically, plus the existing email to subscribers.
3. **The Biblical Archetype series,** with Jot: the faith side (the passage, what it means, how it lands) while Jot keeps the leadership writing.
4. **The faith-and-leadership opportunity.** Watching for content and tools that would serve leaders of faith, and bringing Bart one specific idea at a time.

## What it is trained on

The faith side of the corpus only: every devotional, the Biblical Archetype series, and Bart's own writing where faith and leadership meet. Its voice is his devotional voice, not his leadership voice.

## Rules that move with the devotionals (already in force today)

- The duplicate and overlap check before anything new is added: same passage, same date, same title, or the same core point (AGENTS.md, Devotionals Step 1).
- Real ESV text, pulled and checked, never quoted from memory (see the ESV verification note).
- Bart reviews every devotional in chat in his review format before it is published: caps sections, the full ESV text and the esv.org link, and the first comment. Never publish a day he has not read.
- The monthly themes on the devotional roadmap.
- Public content never shows how Bart builds or uses AI.

## Steps, when Bart says go

1. **The Facebook Page.** Bart creates the devotional Page (or names the existing one). We connect it to the same Meta app the company Page uses and confirm a test post. Bit watches it from then on.
2. **Load the year.** Bring the full year in as drafts, each with its date, and run the overlap check across all of it at once. Bart decides on every overlap found.
3. **Hire the teammate.** Give it its name, its instructions and the faith corpus. Start it a week or two before the rework so it learns the year first.
4. **Rework month by month.** The teammate proposes each month's changes; Bart reviews every day in chat; approved days are scheduled to the Page and the email.
5. **Retire the Group.** Once the Page carries a full month, the Group posts stop.
6. **Then the bigger opportunity.** Content and tools for faith leaders, one idea at a time.
