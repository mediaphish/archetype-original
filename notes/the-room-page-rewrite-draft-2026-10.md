## "Bart Paden"
Bart Paden is the founder of Archetype Original and the author of three books on leadership, organizational behavior, and the human cost of the decisions leaders make every day.

He spent twenty-nine years building a software company from a home office to more than 100 people across multiple locations, and sold it in July 2022. Across thirty-four years in business he has worked inside the leadership rooms of organizations in thirteen industries, not as an outside observer but as the person responsible for outcomes that affected real people.

His work, the books, the research, the writing, and the tools he is building, is designed to give leaders a deeper and more honest understanding of what leading people actually requires. Whether a reader ever engages with him directly or not, the work is built to be useful.

For leaders who want to go further, he works with people directly, from a single conversation to a standing relationship.
