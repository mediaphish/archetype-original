# Auto's CRM: plan

Written 2026-10-25 for Bart's approval.

## What Bart asked for

- "CRM. I assume the Queue is that, so call it that. Then it needs to look and
  work like a CRM ... I want to be able to open the page, know what to do, or
  have an AI I can ask about it quickly. It's not going to just be podcast
  episodes. It's every human I am working with or nurturing towards working
  with."
- "Mobile. I would bet 70% of the time I will be working this system from my
  phone. Every valuable view needs to be planned and designed, not just thrown
  together."
- "We have to be accurate here before we can go more autonomous."

## Where things stand

- The Queue is content waiting on Bart (resurfaces, infographics, quote cards,
  and now team emails). It is not the CRM.
- The CRM is the Clients tab: people, health, transcripts, Gmail, next moves,
  private Archy, the Monday summary. Podcast guests were added 2026-10-25.
- Auto cannot see the CRM at all, so it cannot answer "what do I do today?"

## How a real CRM works, and what that means here

| Part | What it does | Here |
|---|---|---|
| Today | What to do now, ranked, one tap each | Replies owed, emails to approve, follow-ups due, prep for tomorrow's calls, people gone quiet |
| People | Everyone, searchable and filterable | Clients, prospects, podcast guests, mentees, partners, contacts |
| Pipeline | Where each relationship stands | Contact, Conversation booked, Met, In a season, Ongoing, Not now |
| Person | One page per human: history and next step | Timeline of every email, call, transcript, episode and note; quick actions |
| Follow-ups | A next step with a date and an owner | Owned by Bart or a teammate (Cam, Reach, Auto) |
| Sources | How they found Bart | Podcast, referral, talk (e.g. OCC), inquiry, LinkedIn |
| Ask | Questions answered from the CRM itself | "Who should I follow up with this week?" |

## The design, phone first

Bottom bar on a phone, always in thumb reach: **Today · People · Ask · Queue**.

1. **Today.** The first screen. A short ranked list, each item one tap:
   approve, reply, call, text, open. Nothing that does not need Bart.
2. **People.** Search on top, then filter chips (relationship, stage, quiet,
   owner). Each row: name, relationship, stage, last contact, next step.
3. **Person.** Header with call, text and email buttons; the next step; then
   the timeline. Details, notes, transcripts and research one tap away.
4. **Ask.** A chat that knows the CRM (Auto with CRM tools): who, what is
   owed, draft this, move that, log this. Every answer cites the record.
5. **Pipeline.** A board on desktop; on a phone, a stage list with counts.

## Accuracy before autonomy

- Every fact the team states about a person comes from the record.
- Every email is checked against the record before it reaches Bart (built
  2026-10-25).
- Autonomy widens one kind of action at a time once Bart has seen it be right.

## Phases

1. **Today and Ask**: Auto sees the CRM; the Today screen; rename Clients to CRM.
2. **People and Person**, redesigned phone first, plus the pipeline view.
3. **Follow-ups with owners**, sources, and team emails living on each person.
4. **A phone pass over every other view**: Auto chat, Team board, Queue, Podcast.

## Built 2026-10-25 (overnight)

- Phases 1 to 3 are live: the CRM at /ao/crm with Today, People, Pipeline and
  Ask; call, text and email on every person; a next step with a date and an

  as background work; Auto and the team read the CRM before saying anything

- Not yet: phase 4, the phone pass over Auto chat, Team board, Queue and
