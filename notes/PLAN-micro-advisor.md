# Micro Advisor (plan, waiting on Bart)

Bart, 2026-10-28: "I like the review idea. Plan it. It's a 'Micro Advisor' role."

## What it is

A fixed-price second opinion on one decision a leader is facing. They don't hire Bart as an ongoing advisor. They get him for one decision.

Typical decisions: letting someone go, a reorganization, splitting with a partner, a key hire, a contract or deal, how to handle a conflict at the top.

Who it's for: owners and leaders with no board, mentor or peer to think it through with, and leaders who want an outside, honest read before they commit.

## How it works for the buyer

1. **Book and pay** on a Micro Advisor page, through the same Stripe checkout as the guides.
2. **Fill out a short form** about the decision: what's being decided, the options they see, what's at stake, the deadline, and what worries them most.
3. **Pick a call time** from Bart's booking calendar, inside his normal hours.
4. **A 30-minute call** with Bart, who has already read the form.
5. **A written summary within two business days:** what Bart would do and why, the risks, what to watch for, and the first step.

## Bart's part (about an hour per review)

- Read the form before the call (about 10 minutes).
- The call (30 minutes).
- Approve the summary (about 10 minutes).

## The team's part

- **Cam** runs each review as a project: confirms the booking, makes sure the form is complete, and reminds Bart the day before with the form in hand.
- **Jot** drafts the written summary from Bart's call notes. Bart approves it before it goes out.
- **The CRM** records each client, so a good review can lead to a season of advising later, without any pushing.

## Price

Decided by Bart 2026-10-30:

- **$150 for the first five reviews only**, offered to people he knows. In return, each one gives a sentence or two he can quote.
- **$350 for everyone after that.**

## What gets built

- The intake form, which is sent automatically after payment.
- A booking step using Bart's existing booking hours.
- The project flow in Auto for Cam, and the summary draft for Jot.
