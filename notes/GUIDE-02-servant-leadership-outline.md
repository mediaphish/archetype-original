## Chapters

1. **What it is, and what it is not** (Psychology 1, Case 1)
   - They hear servant and think soft. They hear service and think submission.
   - Not the absence of strength. The discipline of it.
   - The insecure leader needs to prove they are in charge. The secure one already knows.
   - Servant leaders still confront, correct and protect standards.
   - Worksheet: *Where I lead from.* Recent pressure moments, what drove my response, what the team got.

2. **How fear sneaks in** (Case 3)
   - Survival disguised as responsibility. It hides inside urgency, performance and control.
   - The team picks up the leader's state before the leader admits it.
   - Fear grips tighter. Service opens the hands.
   - Worksheet: *What fear is telling me.* What it says, what I do because of it, what I would do without it.

3. **Safety before strategy** (Psychology 2)
   - Safety is not comfort. It is consistency.
   - Honesty has to cost less than silence.
   - Protect the voices that tell you the truth. Hold people accountable without humiliation.
   - Worksheet: *The reaction map.* What happens when bad news reaches me, and what my team has learned from it.

4. **Clarity and ownership** (Psychology 3, Logic of Leadership in Case 4)
   - Clarity beats chaos. Empower over control.
   - Autonomy is structured freedom: clear expectations, their call on how.
   - Keeping things foggy is a power play. Servant leaders build capability, not dependency.
   - Worksheet: *The fog check.* What is unclear, who has to come to me, and what I could hand over.

5. **Strength under pressure** (Psychology 4, Serve the Standard in Case 4)
   - Pressure reveals culture. Servant leaders regulate it, they do not remove it.
   - Calm is a competitive advantage. The team mirrors your state before your words.
   - Not slow. Stable. Serve the standard, because people rise to what you model.
   - Points to Holding the Line for the hard conversation, in one line.
   - Worksheet: *The pressure plan.* When to turn the heat up, when to pull it back, and what I model when it is high.

6. **The cost to the servant** (Psychology 5)
   - The hidden cost of being the strong one. Burnout as erosion.
   - The dark side of being needed: ego wearing humility's clothes.
   - Boundaries are structure, not barriers. "I'll walk with you, but I won't carry you."
   - Worksheet: *What I am carrying.* What is mine, what is theirs, and what my yes actually means.

7. **Build it so it outlasts you** (Psychology 6, Why Servant Leadership Disrupts Unaccountable Power, Case 4)
   - Culture is a calendar, not a quote. Rituals, not posters.
   - If the system does not reinforce your values, it will erase them.
   - Why it meets resistance: it works as a stress test on authority that depends on avoiding accountability.
   - Worksheet: *The culture calendar.* How meetings start, how mistakes get handled, who gets credit, what the system actually rewards.
