# The Room: revision brief (October 2026)

For a writing session. Prepared 2026-10-20 from the manuscript at
`ao-knowledge-hq-kit/knowledge/the-room/the-room.md` (about 9,400 words). Line
numbers below refer to that file.

## Why a revision

On 2026-10-19 Bart moved Archetype Original from "Leadership Advisory / the
honest room" to **Work with Bart**: an operator who meets people where they are.
The book was written as the front door to the old advisory offer. It still works
as a book, but its last third sells an offer that no longer exists, in language
Bart has since corrected.

## What to keep

**Chapters 1 to 4 stand on their own and need no structural change.**
- Ch 1, The Room You Think You Have
- Ch 2, They Live Inside the System
- Ch 3, The Cost You Cannot See
- Ch 4, What Leaders Do Instead

The diagnosis (no internal room is fully honest because everyone in it lives
inside the system the leader's decisions shape) is the book's core and is still
true to Bart's thinking. Only fix the years (see below).

## What must change, and why

### 1. "No stake" (the biggest one)
Bart, 2026-10-19: he DOES have a stake in the people he works with. "What's the
point of hiring a consultant that doesn't?" The truth is that he does not live
inside their ecosystem, so he is free of the pressure that shapes everyone who
does.

Passages that say otherwise:
- Line 326 (Ch 5): "one room where the person across from you has no stake in the
  outcome of your decisions and no position inside the system your decisions shape."
- Line 338 (Ch 5): "They have no financial stake in the outcome beyond the
  engagement itself..."
- Line 354 (Ch 5): the Coutu and Kauffman HBR citation, "a thinking partner with
  no stake in the outcome." This is the research's wording; keep the citation
  accurate but frame Bart's own position differently around it.
- Line 21 (front matter summary): "relationship without stake inside the system."

The replacement idea, in wording Bart approved for the site:
> "Everyone around you is shaped by the system you lead, and so is everything they
> tell you. I am not. I have a real stake in how this goes for you, but none of the
> pressure that comes from living inside it."

"Consequence-reduced" (lines 326 and on) can stay: the argument is about
consequence inside the system, not about the advisor not caring.

### 2. Years and history
- "Thirty-three years" at lines 83 (Prologue) and 471 (Ch 6). Now **thirty-four**.
- Line 405: "thirty-plus years running a software engineering firm". The facts:
  Bart built several businesses over 34 years. The longest was a software company
  he grew for twenty-nine years and sold in July 2022, and through it he was
  connected to 13 different industries. Line 471 says "acquired in 2022" and
  "home office to more than 100 people across multiple locations"; those are his
  own words from the book and can stay.
- Since the sale he has spent four years studying business, leadership and AI.
  That is new and could earn a sentence in Ch 6's "My name is Bart Paden" section.
- Do not invent biography. Only facts Bart has written or confirmed.

### 3. "Advisor" and "the advisory"
Ch 6 (lines 391 to 411, 449, 455) and the closing (487 to 514) name the outside
person "an advisor" and the offer "the advisory". The new offer is not called
advisory. Options for the writer to present to Bart:
- keep "advisor" as a general word for the outside person (it is accurate and
  the research uses it), but drop "the advisory" as a product name; or
- shift to the site's language: someone outside your ecosystem, on your side of
  the table.

Line 395 ("not a consultant arriving with a framework") needs care: Bart now
offers Consulting and Fractional roles too. The contrast should be with
frameworks and programs, not with consulting as such.

### 4. The offer and where it points
- Lines 487 to 495 (end of Ch 6) and 508 to 514 (Epilogue) send readers to
  `archetypeoriginal.com/advisory` twice and describe "advisory relationships,
  consulting engagements, and mentorship".
- New destination: `archetypeoriginal.com/work-with-bart`. The old /advisory
  address will forward there, so printed copies keep working.
- The current offer (for reference, not necessarily to print with prices):
  one conversation (90 minutes, one real situation), a season (three months),
  ongoing (a standing relationship), and a rising leaders rate for people early
  in their careers. Plus Consulting and Fractional roles for organizations.
- "The conversation begins with me. Not a process. Not a team. Not an intake form
  that routes you somewhere else. With me." (lines 491 to 493) is strong and
  still true. Keep the spirit.

### 5. Prologue ending (lines 59 to 94)
"The fix is not inside the room. It is outside of it." and "That is what
prepared me for the outside room." still work. Only the years change. Optional:
let the last lines point toward meeting people where they are rather than toward
a single advisory room.

## Bart's voice (read his journal before writing)

Good references: "I Called Myself Retired", "The Org Chart Only Works Until It's
Tested", "Your Tuesday", "Where We Begin" (all in `ao-knowledge-hq-kit/journal/`).
- Specific: dates, counts, names.
- Plain claim, then the proof or the cost.
- Honest about his own story, including failure. No flattery of the reader.
- People first: "develop people, not consume them".
- No em dashes. No AI-signature phrasing. Complete sentences (he will catch
  fragments). Do not repeat the same word inside a short block.
