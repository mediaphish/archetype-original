# AI and Leadership series: image style prompts for ChatGPT

Written 2026-10-25 from the 21 images in the series (~/Downloads/bart-reshare-001 to 021).
Attach one or two real photos of Bart with either prompt so the face stays his.

## The series style

Create a photorealistic, cinematic editorial photograph of the man in the attached photos. Keep his face, build and features exactly as they are: short ginger hair, a full beard that is white with ginger through it, black rectangular glasses, a black smartwatch and a wedding ring. Do not make him younger, thinner or more polished than he is.

Wardrobe: grounded and unfussy. Dark button-up shirts, flannel, a canvas or wool jacket, dark denim, leather boots. Muted colors: black, charcoal, olive, navy, cream.

Setting: [DESCRIBE THE SCENE]. The world he works in: industrial lofts with brick and steel, modern offices with floor-to-ceiling glass over a city, rain-wet city sidewalks, train platforms, airport concourses, busy workspaces, or open nature at the edge of a lake or ridge.

Light: blue hour or golden hour. Warm amber light from lamps, shelves and windows against a cool blue sky. Wet or polished floors that reflect the light. Rich shadows, true blacks, nothing flat or overexposed.

Camera: shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm or 50mm lens at a wide aperture. He is in sharp focus and the background falls off softly. Other people, when present, are softly motion-blurred so he is the still point in a moving world.

Composition: 3:2 landscape (1536 x 1024). He stands, walks or sits in the left or right third of the frame, and the other side is open, quiet space with no clutter, so text can sit there later.

Mood: steady, thoughtful, present. He looks off frame, as if weighing something, never posing or smiling at the camera.

Do not include: any text, signs you can read, logos or brand marks (including on laptops), extra fingers, or a glossy, airbrushed "AI" look. It should read as a real photograph.

## Dystopian round: looking forward

Use the series style above, with the same man, wardrobe, camera, lighting language and open side for text. Change the world around him:

Setting: the near future of work where leadership was handed to machines and people stopped leading. [DESCRIBE THE SCENE, for example: an open office where every desk is a glowing screen and no one looks up; a rain-soaked street under towering digital billboards and hovering delivery drones; a glass boardroom with empty chairs facing a wall of dashboards; a transit platform where everyone wears earpieces and stares at their devices.]

Light: cold cyan and sickly white light from screens and signage, haze and drizzle, harsh contrast. He is the one warm presence in the frame, lit by a single amber source, so he reads as the human in a cold system.

Tone: a quiet warning, not horror. Believable, five to ten years from now, not science fiction costume. No robots with faces, no weapons, no violence, no fire, no ruins. Everyday life that has gone gray and automatic, and one person still paying attention.

Do not include: any text, readable screens, logos or brand marks. It should read as a real photograph.
