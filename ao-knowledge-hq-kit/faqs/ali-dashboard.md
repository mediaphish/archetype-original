It shows you where each of the seven conditions stands, where your view and your team's experience differ, and which patterns are showing up. From the second round on, it shows which direction each condition is drifting. It also points to what to try next where the conditions are weakest.

The honest answer to the second part of the question is that most of this is invisible without structured measurement, especially when you're stressed, busy, or relying on intuition. Leaders tend to interpret what they see generously. ALI doesn't.
