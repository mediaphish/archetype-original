ALI measures the seven conditions a leader creates, the environment people work inside every day:

1. **Clarity: I know what matters.** Priorities, expectations, and what good looks like are plain enough to act on without guessing.
2. **Consistency: people can predict you.** Follow-through and tone are steady from week to week.
3. **Accountability: standards hold for everyone, the leader first.** Problems are addressed directly and early.
4. **Voice: people can tell you the truth.** Disagreement and bad news reach the leader without a price attached.
5. **Growth: people are getting better here.** Real feedback, shared credit, and trust with real decisions.
6. **Support: people are cared for, and pressure is carried fairly.**
7. **Alignment: we are pulling the same direction.** Disagreement is welcome. Drifting apart is not.

Then it measures what those conditions produce. **Trust** is earned over time and can't be declared. **Commitment** is whether people choose to stay and give their best. **Drift** is the direction the conditions are moving from one round to the next, the early warning before something slides into damage.

Leaders and their teams answer the same questions, so ALI also shows where the leader's view and the team's experience don't match.

Most leadership tools measure emotions. ALI measures the environment leadership creates. That's a different thing entirely, and it's far more predictive. Conditions shape behavior long before results appear. ALI makes those conditions visible while there's still time to act on them.
