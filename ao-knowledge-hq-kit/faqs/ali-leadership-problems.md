Nearly all of them trace back to one or more weakened conditions.

Misalignment points to Clarity or Alignment. Conflict that never gets resolved points to Accountability or Voice. Turnover points to Support, Growth, or Consistency. Slow execution points to Clarity or Consistency. Disengagement points to Voice, Growth, or Support. Problems that surface too late point to Voice. Reactive leadership points to Consistency. Strategy breakdown points to Clarity or Alignment.

Most organizations treat the surface problem. ALI reveals the root condition. That's the difference between solving something and managing it indefinitely.
