## Scripture

**1 Timothy 4:4–5 (ESV)**

## Reflection

Some leaders are suspicious of anything good. A long lunch feels like lost time. A bonus feels like something to apologize for. A good meal with the team gets cut short because there is always more work. They would not call it a conviction, but it works like one, and it slowly teaches everyone around them that enjoying anything is a little irresponsible.

Paul does not leave room for that. For everything created by God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thanksgiving. Everything created by God. The good things in a leader's life are not traps to be avoided. They were made, and they were made good.

The condition is in how they are received. Received with thanksgiving. The same table can be met with guilt, with indifference or with thanks, and only one of those treats it as a gift. Thankfulness does not make a thing good. It recognizes that it already was.

For it is made holy by the word of God and prayer. The ordinary meal, the rest, the celebration all become something set apart when they are received this way. What could have been simple consumption becomes an act of worship.

Leaders set the tone for how good things are received. A leader who can sit at a full table and give thanks, without guilt and without excess, gives their people permission to do the same. Refusing every good gift can look like discipline. The text calls for something better, receiving it with thanksgiving.

## Practical Application

- Receive one good thing fully, without apologizing for it, and give thanks for it.
- Pray before the next meal you share with your team.
- Notice where you treat enjoyment as something to feel guilty about.

## Takeaways

- Everything created by God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thanksgiving.
- Thankfulness does not make a gift good. It recognizes that it already was.

## Closing Thought

The table was good before anyone sat down, and thanks is how it was received.
