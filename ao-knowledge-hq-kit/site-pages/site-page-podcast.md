# The Archetype Original Podcast

Leadership is not a title. It is a human condition. This show chases it everywhere it lives, in the boardroom and the sanctuary, the corner office and the break room.

Two kinds of episodes: solo conversations drawn from 34 years of building companies and growing people, and guest conversations with no boxes, no titles required, just people who have carried weight and have something true to say about it.
